# Recruiting cohorts and robust-efficiency extension
This third stage is explicitly requested after the first two exploratory searches. No new holdout is claimed, and current Florida/Houston ranks do not select a model.

New data: thirteen annual SportsDataverse/247 recruit-level files (2014-2026). Compare six treatments: none; the previously used published four-class proxy; equal weighting of the current and three prior classes; developmental weights .25/.75/1/1; exclude the current freshman class (three older classes); lagged four classes (one through four years earlier). Signed-class membership is not verified active-roster membership. Do not claim freshmen are all redshirted or that this identifies actual redshirts.

Use valid 2-5 star recruit records to compute weighted average stars and blue-chip fraction. Reject unrated/invalid star values, deduplicate recruit/year IDs and report coverage. Avoid raw-grade anomalies (some source grades are on inconsistent scales). Standardize each metric across that year's FBS teams and average the two standardized components; restandardize that composite. Missing teams receive zero (FBS mean) with a visible flag. Standardize the published talent proxy as well so prior weights are comparable. Recruiting enters only the preseason scoring prior.

Prior weights: 0 for none, 2/4 equivalent points per cross-team standard deviation for each of the five recruiting proxies. Twelve base designs: carry .35/.65 x prior penalty 2/4 x (raw+uniform, raw+mismatch, bounded surprise+uniform). Total 132 rating designs. Four bundles: score; score+Elo; score+EPA+special teams+Elo; the latter plus opponent-adjusted non-explosive EPA and turnover control. Two logistic penalties give 1,056 candidates. Non-explosive EPA is not a verified garbage-time filter. Turnover control is negative turnovers per game, jointly adjusted for opposing units. No hand-placed school corrections.

Use unchanged nested previous-three-year log-loss selection with positive strength coefficients and scalar rankings. Compare all generations and both preliminary stages; report Brier/accuracy/log loss, week bootstrap, weak-opponent and early/late slices, plus timestamp-valid FPI. Audit whether reduced or excluded freshman weights actually help on earlier-year validation, not just the current ranking.

Investigated game-roster data are excluded from predictive fitting: historical starter flags are absent in 2018 and 2023 and coverage is inconsistent. Current-year roster unions and did_not_play flags cannot establish preseason playing availability. No fabricated returning-production or transfer correction is substituted.
