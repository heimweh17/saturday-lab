Sources, rights & limitations

What Saturday Lab uses, what it owns and what the published numbers mean.

Independent project

Saturday Lab is an independent, noncommercial personal analytics project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the NCAA, ESPN, any conference or any university.

Names and marks

Team names, logos, conference names and related marks belong to their respective owners. They appear for identification and analytical commentary.

Data sources

Historical schedules, team membership, team box scores and scoring by period come from SportsDataverse releases derived from ESPN records. Future schedules use a public ESPN team-schedule endpoint. Play-efficiency data come from the public cfbfastR pipeline. Recruiting information is a four-signed-class proxy.

Forecast limits

Predictions are estimates, not guarantees or betting advice. The model lacks verified injuries, future depth charts, complete transfer-adjusted rosters, weather and betting markets. Dated snapshots do not update automatically.

Reuse and audit

The source code, calculations, validation results and machine-readable datasets are published for inspection. Consult the repository license and provenance file before reuse.

Privacy

The static site has no accounts, comments, advertising profiles or first-party user database. GitHub Pages and linked external services may apply their own logging and privacy policies.
