Introduction

Saturday Lab is a college football data desk for following teams, games and the national picture. You do not need to know the model to use it.

01

Follow the games

Scores & schedule shows recent finals and upcoming forecasts. Live game checks run throughout the week and more often on Saturdays. Open any matchup for the full game page.

02

Read the rankings

The national ranking is a weekly snapshot published on Monday. It stays fixed during the week, so a Friday result can appear before it enters the next ranking.

03

Look back or ahead

Team pages collect results, statistics and future win chances. Historical ranking URLs let you revisit what the field looked like in an earlier week.
